DISCOVA

Know if you can get in — before you go.

Other apps tell you where to go. DISCOVA tells you whether you'll actually get inside — scoring how accessible a cafe, mall or park is for wheelchair users, seniors, parents with strollers and people with sensory needs.

Download APK Back to projects

The problem

For millions of people, the question isn't "where should I go?" — it's "will I be able to get in, use the washroom, and feel comfortable once I'm there?" Today that information is pure guesswork. You find out a place has stairs only after you've arrived.

DISCOVA fixes that. It combines the discovery feel of Instagram and Google Maps with real accessibility intelligence — so you know, before you leave home, exactly what to expect. It's built for people with disabilities and for parents, seniors and anyone who values comfort.

Core features

AI accessibility score

Gemini reads photos and reviews to rate ramps, lifts, washrooms and parking automatically.

Real places near you

Live Google places with a rating ≥ 3.9 and real photos — no fake listings.

Crowd & quiet meter

See how busy a place is and the best time to visit when it's calmest.

Universal Comfort Index

Parking, walking distance, seating and calm — scored for everyone, not just PwD.

Hidden gems & weather-aware

Surfaces underrated spots and suggests indoor places when it's raining.

Community reviews

Real users rate accessibility themselves, keeping every score honest and current.

Visit nudge

Move 3km away from a place and DISCOVA gently asks you to rate it.

Gamification

Coins (2× for PwD), challenges, leaderboard and stories to reward contributions.

Who it's for

People with disabilities — wheelchair, visual, hearing and cognitive — and everyday users like parents with strollers and seniors. Inclusive by design: value for everyone.
